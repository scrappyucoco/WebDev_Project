Our Background

Binanua-an holds an important place in the history of Igbaras. Historical accounts describe the area as an early settlement and townsite, positioned near hills and natural surroundings that helped shape the development of the community.

Over time, the community continued to grow as Igbaras developed into the municipality known today. Binanua-an was formally recognized as a barrio of Igbaras through Republic Act No. 2901 in 1960, giving the community a clearer place within the municipality's local history.

Igbaras itself is widely recognized for its mountains, rivers, caves, waterfalls, and other natural attractions. Places such as Mount Napulak, Nadsadjan Falls, Timapok Falls, and several caves have helped establish the municipality as a destination for people interested in nature and outdoor experiences.

Situated within this natural setting, Binanua-an Eco Camp and the Imbalayan Binanua-an Igbaras Resort represent the kind of countryside experience that allows visitors to slow down, enjoy the surroundings, and appreciate the environment. The area provides a setting where visitors can step away from busy urban spaces and spend time closer to nature.

Today, the spirit of the place is centered on hospitality, appreciation for the environment, and creating memorable experiences for visitors. More than simply being a place to stay, the resort is part of a community and landscape shaped by the natural beauty and history of Igbaras.
