Our Story & Tradition

Preserving Authentic Ilonggo Flavors

From a beloved neighborhood batchoyan to a multi-branch culinary destination in Iloilo City.

Bringing Back the Classic Taste

Founded on a passion for authentic local recipes, Alicia's Special Batchoy is dedicated to reviving traditional Ilonggo noodle soup flavors. Our broth is simmered slowly using bone marrow, pork, and natural spices, delivering that unmistakable savory comfort that Ilonggos and tourists love.

Beyond our signature noodle soups, we operate dual-concept locations such as our popular Alicia's Batchoy Restaurant & Car Wash in Jaro, allowing patrons to enjoy hot meals while having their vehicles serviced.

Our Core Pillars of Excellence

What makes dining at Alicia's a memorable experience across all 4 locations.

Authentic Ingredients

Fresh noodles (pancit, sotanghon, miswa, bihon), crispy chicharon, scallions, and steamed puto made fresh daily.

4 Prime Branches

Conveniently located in Jaro, La Paz, Arevalo, and Smallville to serve the community across Iloilo City.

Weatherproof Hospitality

Open rain or shine, supported by digital Grab delivery integration so hot batchoy is always within reach.
